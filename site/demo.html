Windows Full offline Tutor

For 64-bit Windows computers. Includes the Granite 3.3 2B model and local llama.cpp runtime.

  1. Download the 1.68 GB installer.
  2. Run the setup file and follow the installation prompts.
  3. Open AI Shiksha from the desktop or Start menu.
Download for Windows SHA-256: 879ED3E9…C0FB84A9
Android Full offline Tutor

For Android 9 or newer. Includes the mobile Granite model and requires about 1.5 GB of free storage.

  1. Download the 702 MB APK.
  2. Allow installation from your browser or Files app.
  3. Keep the app open while the first Tutor question prepares the model.

iPhone and iPad Browser app available

The installable Safari PWA includes the course and ML Studio. The native offline Tutor awaits Apple signing and TestFlight.

  1. Open the browser demo in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch AI Shiksha from its Home Screen icon.
Open iPhone/iPad app Native Tutor package: Xcode-ready, not yet distributed through TestFlight.
Mac Browser app available

Use the Safari or Chrome PWA for the course, projects, progress, PDF export, and ML Studio.

  1. Open the browser demo.
  2. In Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Launch AI Shiksha from the Dock or Applications.
Open Mac browser app Native macOS installer and offline Generative AI Tutor are not currently available.
